About Axiom Cooperation

Axiom Cooperation is an individual/sole-proprietor company founded and operated by Ali Raza, based in Islamabad, Pakistan. The company is organized into five divisions — Trading, Finance, Marketing, Technology, and Ventures — each building focused products and services over time.

The Trading and Technology divisions are currently active, with Axiom Trading Journal as the first live product: a free web-based trade journal and performance analytics tool for Forex and financial traders. Finance, Marketing, and Ventures are in active development toward asset management, brand and marketing services, and startup investing respectively.
